# Julia (Female) Cutting Diet Plan

## Diet Overview

This cutting plan is designed to support fat loss while preserving lean muscle mass. The structure remains simple, practical, and high-protein, with moderate carbohydrates around training and controlled fat intake throughout the day. The plan is organized around five training days and two recovery days, with slightly lower carbohydrates on rest days while keeping protein high and energy intake close to target.

## Nutrition Targets

| Target            |                                      Value |
| ----------------- | -----------------------------------------: |
| Goal              | Fat loss while preserving lean muscle mass |
| Calories          |                              1600 kcal/day |
| Protein           |                                      140 g |
| Carbohydrates     |                                      160 g |
| Fat               |                                       45 g |
| Water             |                              2.5–3.5 L/day |
| Training Schedule |              5 training days + 2 rest days |

## Weekly Meal Plan

### Saturday — Training Day

#### Breakfast

- Ingredients: 2 whole eggs (100 g), 3 egg whites (100 g), oats (40 g), skimmed milk (200 ml), vegetables (200 g)
- Purpose:
  - High-quality protein
  - Sustainable energy
  - Healthy fats
  - Slow-digesting carbohydrates
- Macros: 430 kcal | 31 g protein | 36 g carbohydrates | 13 g fat

#### Snack

- Ingredients: Greek yogurt (170 g), almonds (15 g)
- Purpose:
  - Control hunger
  - Maintain amino acid availability
- Macros: 200 kcal | 18 g protein | 10 g carbohydrates | 9 g fat

## General Notes

- Whey protein is optional and can be used when convenience or recovery is a priority.
- Whole-food protein sources should remain the base of the plan whenever possible.
- Water intake should remain at 2.5–3.5 liters daily, with extra fluids around training.
- Meal timing can be adjusted by 1–2 hours to fit your schedule as long as the same food structure is maintained.
- Weigh cooked meats, rice, pasta, and potatoes for the most accurate nutrition tracking.
- Vegetables should be included at each main meal and used freely to improve volume and micronutrients.
- Consistency over perfection is the priority; the plan works best when repeated week after week.
- Track body weight, waist measurement, energy, and training performance each week to confirm whether the plan is supporting progress.
