# Bedo – Cutting Diet Plan (≈2400 kcal Target)

## Weekly Macro Targets
- Calories: ~2,400 kcal
- Protein: ~250–265 g
- Carbohydrates: ~210–230 g
- Fat: ~55–59 g
- Water: 3–4 L/day

**General Notes**
- Meat, chicken, fish, rice, pasta, and potatoes are weighed **after cooking**.
- Vegetables and salads are weighed **raw**.
- Drink **3–4 liters of water daily**.
- Black coffee or tea without sugar is allowed.
- Post-workout protein should prioritize whole foods (chicken, fish, or lean beef) instead of whey whenever possible.
