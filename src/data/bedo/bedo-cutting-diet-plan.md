# Bedo – Cutting Diet Plan (2400 kcal Target)

## Weekly Macro Targets
- Calories: ~2,400 kcal
- Protein: ~220–235 g
- Carbohydrates: ~205–215 g
- Fat: ~63–66 g
- Water: 3–4 L/day

**General Notes**
- Meat, chicken, fish, rice, pasta, and potatoes are weighed **after cooking**.
- Vegetables and salads are weighed **raw**.
- Black coffee or tea allowed without sugar (zero-calorie sweetener OK).
- Post-workout protein priority: whole-food animal protein (chicken/fish/beef) over whey.
- Follow progressive overload with proper exercise technique.
