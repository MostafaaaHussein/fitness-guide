# Bedo Cutting Diet Plan

## Diet Overview

This cutting plan is designed to preserve maximum muscle mass while creating a controlled calorie deficit. The structure remains professional and practical, with high protein intake, moderate carbohydrates around training, and balanced fats throughout the week. The plan is built around five training days and two recovery days, with slightly lower carbohydrates on rest days while keeping protein high and calories close to target.

## Nutrition Targets

| Target            |                                        Value |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------: |
| Goal              | Cutting while preserving maximum muscle mass |
| Calories          |                                2400 kcal/day |
| Protein           |                                        250 g |
| Carbohydrates     |                                        200 g |
| Fat               |                                         65 g |
| Water             |                                    3–4 L/day |
| Training Schedule |                5 training days + 2 rest days |

## Weekly Meal Plan

### Saturday — Training Day

#### Breakfast

- Ingredients: 5 egg whites (250 g), 2 whole eggs (100 g), oats (80 g), skimmed milk (300 ml), vegetables (200 g)
- Purpose:
  - High-quality protein
  - Slow-digesting carbohydrates
  - Healthy fats
  - Long-lasting energy
- Macros: 600 kcal | 55 g protein | 45 g carbohydrates | 16 g fat

#### Snack

- Ingredients: Greek yogurt (250 g), almonds (20 g)
- Purpose:
  - Maintain amino acid availability
  - Reduce hunger
  - Improve recovery
- Macros: 220 kcal | 25 g protein | 15 g carbohydrates | 10 g fat

#### Lunch

- Ingredients: grilled chicken breast (220 g cooked), cooked rice (250 g), olive oil (10 ml), salad vegetables (300 g)
- Purpose:
  - Main protein source
  - Glycogen replenishment
  - Micronutrients
- Macros: 720 kcal | 72 g protein | 50 g carbohydrates | 18 g fat

#### Pre-Workout

- Ingredients: banana (150 g), rice cakes (30 g)
- Purpose:
  - Stable energy
  - Better performance
  - Reduced fatigue
- Macros: 230 kcal | 8 g protein | 40 g carbohydrates | 3 g fat

#### Post-Workout

- Ingredients: whey protein (45 g), cooked rice (200 g)
- Purpose:
  - Fast recovery
  - Muscle protein synthesis
  - Glycogen restoration
- Macros: 350 kcal | 45 g protein | 35 g carbohydrates | 5 g fat

#### Dinner

- Ingredients: cottage cheese (300 g), mixed vegetables (250 g)
- Purpose:
  - High protein
  - Satiety
  - Overnight muscle recovery
- Macros: 320 kcal | 45 g protein | 20 g carbohydrates | 12 g fat

Daily total: 2440 kcal | 250 g protein | 205 g carbohydrates | 64 g fat

## General Notes

- Whey protein is optional and can be used when convenience or recovery is a priority.
- Whole-food protein sources should remain the base of the plan whenever possible.
- Water intake should remain at 3–4 liters daily, with extra fluids around training.
- Meal timing can be adjusted by 1–2 hours to fit your schedule as long as the same food structure is maintained.
- Weigh cooked meats, rice, pasta, and potatoes for the most accurate nutrition tracking.
- Vegetables should be included at each main meal and used freely to improve volume and micronutrients.
- Consistency over perfection is the priority; the plan works best when repeated week after week.
- Track body weight, waist measurement, energy, and training performance each week to confirm whether the plan is supporting progress.
